
Background:
============
- I am sure you know this but to refresh:
 - Dispatch : Act of sending instructions by output of decoder to Reservation Stations
 - Issue : Act of sending instructions(uops) from RS to functional unit
 - Retire : Rate at which instructions(uops) retire/commit from ROB

- Life of a Store:
 - Compute Address
 - Get Coherence Permission
 - Get data in cache



Problem Statement:
==================
 For a Load Store Queue, the 2 KPIs that matter are :
  (1) Size : To keep up with increasing ROB (Instruction Window sizes)
  (2) Search Bandwidth : Issue width keeps increasing in modern processors
This paper sets out to optimize the above 2 KPIs

Constraints:
============
 It needs to to solve (at least) the following 3 problems:
  (1) Load queue is searched by stores to detect mis-speculation in store-load reoredering (i.e, hoisting of loads above stores with RAW hazard)
  (2) Store queue is search by loads to do store-load forwarding (non-speculative path)
  (3) Detect Load-Load reorderings that are written to by other cores (remote intervening stores) (informed by the coherence protocol)


Unique Solution:
=================
 (1) Reduce Search by bandwidth by only "load scanning store queue" for those instructions specifically marked to do so
     This will however require "store scanning load queue" to detect mis-speculation

 (2) Segmented load-store queue to allow it to scale better
